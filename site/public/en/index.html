Product

Live product

Fuchidori

Fuchidori adds a frame and a line of shooting information (camera, lens, exposure, date and more) to your photos and videos. It is a web app (PWA) that runs in your phone's browser — nothing to install.

  • Open the app
  • About page
  • Privacy policy
  • Loading photos and videos, reading shooting information, compositing the frame and exporting all happen inside your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Location data (GPS) is removed from exported photos.
  • For photos without EXIF, you can type the camera, lens and more yourself. Typed values are kept only on your device, as suggestions for next time.
  • The about pages are available in Japanese and English.
