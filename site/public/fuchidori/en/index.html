Photo frame & caption

Frame your photos
with their story.

Fuchidori reads the camera, lens, exposure and date from your photo, then lays out a clean border and caption, ready to share. It runs in your browser: nothing to install, no account.

Open the app Free · No sign-up · Interface in Japanese
Works

Same photo, different frame, new mood

Pick a ratio, margin, colour and typeface. Text size and position adapt to the photo automatically.

Sample images are illustrations framed with Fuchidori, not real photographs. The shooting details are sample values.

Features

Just the tools you need to finish a photo

Fine-tune everything if you like, but the moment you open a photo, it is already laid out and ready to save.

  • 01

    Shooting details, automatically

    Camera, lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focal length and date are read from the photo, including FUJIFILM film simulation names. Anything missing can be typed in.

  • 02

    Ready for social media

    Keep the original ratio or choose 4:5, 3:4, 2:3, 1:1, 9:16, 16:9 or 1.91:1 for Instagram, X, Threads and TikTok.

  • 03

    Margin, colour, border

    Five margin steps from none to wide. With no margin, the caption sits on the photo. Nine background colours and five border weights.

  • 04

    14 type styles

    Latin faces in the style of Helvetica, Futura, DIN, Didot and more (including bold weights), plus a Japanese face. Adjust size and letter spacing.

  • 05

    Arrange the caption

    One to four lines. Drag items to choose which line they appear on. Pick a separator (, · / — | space) and a date format.

  • 06

    Video, too

    Add the same frame and caption to videos up to 60 seconds (where the browser supports video export).

Your photos never leave your device

Everything from opening to exporting happens inside the browser on your device.

Privacy policy

  • No uploads. There is no mechanism that sends photos or videos to a server.
  • No analytics, no ads. No cookies either.
  • Location removed on export. Shooting details (EXIF) are kept; GPS is stripped.
  • Settings stay on this device. Next time, you start from your last frame.
How to

Three steps

  1. Choose a photo

    Tap the frame in the middle to pick a photo or video. On a computer you can also drag and drop or paste.

  2. Adjust

    Choose ratio, margin, caption and typeface, switching tabs while you watch the result.

  3. Save or share

    On iPhone, use the share sheet to save the image or post it directly. On a computer, choose where to save it.

Install

Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience

No App Store needed. Launch it from its icon, without the browser bar.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow)
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the app in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right
  3. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”
Specs

Requirements & output

Browsers
Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer (latest versions recommended)
Photos
Exported as JPEG (4096 px on the long edge). EXIF is kept; location is removed
Videos
Up to 60 seconds. Exported as MP4 (1920 px on the long edge), or MOV / WebM depending on the browser
Language
The app interface is currently in Japanese
Price
Free. No account required
