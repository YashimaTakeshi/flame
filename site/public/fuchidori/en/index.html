A web app that frames your photos with their shooting details

Pick a photo and Fuchidori reads the camera, lens and exposure, then sets them in small type in the margin. Choose a ratio and a colour, save, and post. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Open the app Free to use · Interface in Japanese

It reads what your camera recorded and sets it in three lines

Date, camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed and ISO come straight from the file. Anything missing can be typed in on the spot.

Line 1Date six formats, e.g. 2025.05.24
Line 2Camera set in bold
Line 3Lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO

For FUJIFILM photos it also reads the film simulation (CLASSIC CHROME and so on). Drag items between lines to rearrange them, and use one to four lines.

Straight out of camera, then framed

Same photo, different frame, different mood

Eight ratios, nine background colours and fourteen type styles. Set the margin to none and the caption sits on the photo itself.

Export straight to the shape each platform wants

Also: original ratio, 3:4, 2:3 and 1.91:1.

Your photos go nowhere

From opening to exporting, everything happens inside the browser on your phone or computer. Fuchidori’s server has no way to receive a photo.

Pick, adjust, save

  1. 1Pick a photo

    Tap the frame in the middle to choose a photo or video. On a computer, drag it in or paste it.

  2. 2Adjust

    Switch between the tabs at the bottom to set ratio, margin, colour and caption.

  3. 3Save

    On iPhone, save to Photos from the share sheet, or send it straight to Instagram or X.

Open the app

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. On iPhone, use Safari’s Share button and choose “Add to Home Screen”. On Android, use Chrome’s menu and choose “Add to Home screen”.

If you like it, pass it on. Links show up with a sample image.

Requirements and output

Browsers
Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer (latest versions recommended)
Photos
Exported as JPEG, 4,096 px on the long edge. Shooting details are kept; location is removed
Videos
Up to 60 seconds, with the same frame and caption (MP4 at 1,920 px on the long edge; MOV or WebM on some browsers)
Language
The app interface is in Japanese
Price
Free. No account needed
